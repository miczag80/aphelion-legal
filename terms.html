Privacy Policy

Aphelion Uploader is a private tool used by the owner of the Aphelion channel to publish the channel's own videos to its own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it accesses

  • Basic profile information of the connected Aphelion account (account ID and display name), used only to confirm it is posting to the right account.
  • Permission to upload videos, captions and titles to that account.

What it stores

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a private folder readable by the owner alone. A local log records which of the channel's videos were uploaded and when. Nothing is stored on any server operated by the tool.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, read or store data about other users, viewers, followers or commenters.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use any data for advertising or analytics.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the connected platform's app settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services). Revoking access makes the stored token useless; the owner deletes the local token file at the same time.
